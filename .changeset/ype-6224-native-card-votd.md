BibleCard and Verse of the Day draw scripture with native text, on the same passage path as BibleReader and BibleTextView (YPE-6224). Footnotes and verse numbers match the reader. A missing passage, a failed request, and a lost connection use the reader messages. Failed and offline requests can be retried. `dom` on both remains accepted but is deprecated and ignored — it will be removed in the next major.
